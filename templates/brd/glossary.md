# Glossary Template

## Purpose

Map source-language business terms (often Arabic) to stable English canonical names used across `ai_specs/brd/`. Build this early and keep it updated when new domain terms appear.

## Source Language Notes

- Primary source language: Arabic | English | mixed
- OCR / extraction quality notes:
- Spec canonical language: English (default) | bilingual

## Term Map

|-------------|-------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Example: مزود | provider | vendor (avoid) | Section … |

## Naming Rules

- Use canonical English names in feature files, app-surface files, headings, and cross-links.
- Keep source terms here and in short quoted snippets when meaning is ambiguous.
- Do not rename a canonical term mid-project without updating this glossary and existing links.
- If two source terms map to one concept, pick one canonical name and list the other under aliases.
